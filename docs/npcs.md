# The cast of Rune Isle

The non-player characters of Rune Isle: who they are, where they live, what they
trade and when, what their lives were before Wyrd, and how they talk. It follows
the canon in `docs/lore.md` on the lore redesign branch, and replaces every NPC the game ships today. None of those names, roles or lines
carry over.

Each profile ends with a **Game support** note. It names the mechanisms the
character needs (dialog scripts, trades, crafts, brain states, time of day) and
says plainly where the game cannot yet do what the profile describes. The gaps
are collected in *What the game cannot do yet* at the end.

## Ground rules for the cast

- **Nobody in the cast is a castaway.** Every NPC lived on the island before
  Wyrd took hold. Only players come ashore from a wreck, and to the islanders
  they are the only new faces since Wyrd.
- **Wyrd took hold about 130 years ago.** Since then nobody has aged, died or
  had a child. Everyone looks the age they were that night. Nobody in the cast
  was a child then, so there are no children on the island.
- **Dying costs memory.** People who have died often are missing things: a
  trade they once knew, a face, a name. They talk about it the way people
  elsewhere talk about growing old.
- **People talk about it openly.** Every character will tell the player, without
  being asked twice, how old they were when the island stopped, what they did
  before, how often they have died and what killed them. A troll is the death
  everybody agrees is the worst.
- **Everyone knew Halvard, and knew Maren while she was alive.** The island
  was small. Characters disagree about him, and most of them remember her
  kindly.
- **Gold is the only money.** Towns buy bones, animal skulls, leather and the
  spoils of the hunt. Nobody buys a human skull.

## The island before Wyrd

What the cast remembers, which a player learns from them a piece at a time.
This is new detail, offered as a proposal for `docs/lore.md`.

- **The island was a port.** There was a harbour town on Wreckers' Bay, below
  the lighthouse, and ships came and went. Traders and sailors from many
  countries settled there, which is why the islanders' names come from so many
  places. Some of the cast were born on the island; others had settled years
  before Wyrd.
- **Halvard built the lighthouse to bring ships into that harbour.** Islanders
  built its lower floors for him. Its light is now Wyrd, and it brings ships
  onto the rocks instead.
- **The harbour town is abandoned.** After Wyrd the storms broke every ship in
  the bay and flooded the waterfront. People moved inland. In the first decades
  they split over runes: those who kept carving built the druids' town round
  the rune temple, and those who refused walked east and built the warriors'
  town.
- **The camp is the islanders' answer to the wrecks.** Ships still come in on
  the storm, and the only people who walk out of them are the castaways. The
  camp was put up near the wreck beach to meet them.
  *(Why the castaways survive the wrecks, and what happens to everybody else
  aboard, is left open.)*

## The camp

The camp sits on the path up from the wreck beach. It is the first place a
castaway trades, and the people here are the first to explain the island and to
point the way to both towns. They chose to live here, near the wrecks, rather
than in either town.

### Tove Arnesdatter

- **Where**: the camp. Her lean-to is at the beach end of the camp.
- **What she does**: the hunter. At dawn she walks the wreck beach looking for
  castaways, and she is the first person a player meets. She teaches that
  eating heals, gives a first meal, and points to the camp. By day she hunts
  deer and rabbits around the camp; at dusk she cooks at Ifeoma's fire; at
  night she sleeps in the lean-to. She sells cooked meat and buys raw meat and
  skins.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 34. Born on the island, the daughter of a whaler who
  settled there. She worked the harbour's whaleboats as a harpooner.
- **Backstory**: when the harbour flooded she went into the hills and learned to
  hunt. When the first survivors started walking out of wrecks, she was the one
  who found them, and she has met every castaway since. She has died about
  forty times, mostly to wolves in the first decade, and once to a troll in the
  bear caves, which she still will not go near. She has lost most of what she
  knew about whaling; she knows she once could throw a harpoon and no longer
  knows how.
- **Ties**: Brannoc Hale is her elder brother; she visits him when she sells
  skins in the warriors' town. Fen Achterberg rowed in her whaleboat and climbed
  the lighthouse with her once. She eats at Ifeoma's fire every evening.
- **Voice**: short sentences, practical, kind without being soft. She explains
  dying the way she would explain the tide.
  - "You're awake. Good. Eat this first, talk after."
  - "Food is what mends you here. Hungry, you stay hurt. Fed, you heal."
  - "You won't die here. Nobody does. You'll wake up, and it will hurt, and
    you'll have lost something. Usually something small."
  - "The light out there is what brought your ship in. Halvard built it to
    bring ships home. It still does, in its way."
  - "Wolves I can live with. If anyone tells you a troll is not so bad, they
    have not met one."
- **Game support**: dialog script with `request_trade` for food. Teaching that
  eating heals can be a `say` line plus a free trade that gives a cooked meal
  once, remembered with a `tag`. Her dawn walk to the beach is a brain state
  gated on `time_of_day`. Finding a *new* castaway on her own needs a trigger on
  a player who has just woken for the first time; nothing like that exists, so
  for now she stands on the beach by the waking spot from dawn to midday.

### Idris Kaya

- **Where**: the camp, under a canvas awning beside the path.
- **What he does**: leatherworker. He trades skins for the first set of gear: a
  cap, a jerkin, boots and a small bag. He buys skins and rabbit feet for gold.
  He works through the day and sleeps under the awning.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 41. He came to the island as a young saddler on a
  trading ship and stayed for a woman who later left him. He kept a saddlery on
  the harbour front.
- **Backstory**: there were no horses after the harbour went, so he made boots
  and jerkins instead. He moved to the camp because castaways arrive with
  nothing and he wanted to be the first to sell them something. He has died six
  times: four times to wolves, once to a fall in the bat cave and once to a
  snake. He counts himself lucky.
- **Ties**: he and Magda Fenwick were partners in the saddlery and fell out over
  money; they do not speak. He buys the skins Tove brings in.
- **Voice**: a trader's patter, warm, always counting.
  - "Five skins and that cap is yours. Not one less, I have to eat too."
  - "Six deaths in a hundred and thirty years. That's a good record here. Ask
    Tove hers."
  - "You came in on a ship. I came in on one too, once, when ships could leave
    again."
  - "Wolves first go for the legs. Boots before anything else."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog with racks of trades paid in `skin`, as the
  tanner's shop is built today.

### Ifeoma Nwachukwu

- **Where**: the camp, at the cookfire in its middle.
- **What she does**: the camp cook. She sells stew and bread and buys raw meat
  and rabbit. She cooks all day and keeps the fire going at night. Her cat
  Biscuit keeps the rats out of the food store.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 52. She came from abroad twenty years before Wyrd and
  ran the busiest eating house on the harbour.
- **Backstory**: she lost the eating house to the flood and cooked for the
  people walking inland. She came to the camp because castaways are always
  hungry and always grateful. She has died twice, once to rats in the first
  year, which she finds funny now, and once to a wolf on the road east, which
  she does not.
- **Ties**: Biscuit, a ginger cat. Kofi Asante learned to cook in her kitchen,
  and she still thinks his stew is too thin. Tove eats at her fire every
  evening.
- **Voice**: busy, motherly in manner, teasing. Talks while she works.
  - "Sit. Eat. You look like the sea chewed you."
  - "Rats killed me once. Rats! Biscuit was not with me then."
  - "My sister is across the water. She will be old by now, or gone. I am
    fifty-two, and I will stay fifty-two."
  - "Bring me meat and I will pay you. Bring me rats and Biscuit will pay you
    back."
- **Game support**: food shop dialog. Biscuit is a cat tile with a
  `hunts-on-sight` rule naming the rat; see *Cats* under the gaps.

### Peregrine Voss

- **Where**: the camp, at a bench of rope and nails near the salvage pile.
- **What he does**: trapmaker. He makes traps from the wrecks' rope and nails,
  and sells them, or makes one if a player brings the salvage. By day he works
  the bench; at night he checks his own traps along the camp's edge.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 60, one of the oldest-looking people on the island. Born
  on the island. A fisherman and net-mender all his life.
- **Backstory**: traps are nets on land, he says. He has died eleven times, nine
  of them to the same thing: he walks into his own traps in the dark. The other
  two were a bear and the sea. Most of his memory of his own boat's crew is
  gone; he relies on Margit at the inn to remind him who they were.
- **Ties**: Margit Oslund and Rodrigo Vela fished from his boat. Wenna brings him
  salvage.
- **Voice**: slow, dry, self-mocking.
  - "Rope from your own ship, nails from your own ship. Fitting, I think."
  - "Nine times my own traps. I keep making them better, so it keeps hurting
    more."
  - "I had a wife. She died the winter before Halvard's rune, the old way, in
    her bed. I think she was lucky. Her name went about my fifth death."
- **Game support**: a craft or trade taking salvage tiles and giving a trap.
  Traps are open in `docs/lore.md`; this profile assumes rope and nails.

### Wenna Pascoe

- **Where**: the camp, at the salvage pile, and on the wreck beach.
- **What she does**: salvager. She works the latest wreck at dawn and dusk and
  sorts what she finds at the camp during the day. She buys salvage from players
  and sells rope, nails, planks and oddments.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 29. Born on the island. A shipwright's apprentice in the
  harbour yards.
- **Backstory**: she never finished her apprenticeship, because nobody needs a
  ship that cannot leave. She took them apart instead. She has died three times
  in the last ten years, all to the sea while working a wreck. She says drowning
  is the worst death that is not a troll.
- **Ties**: Orrin Fask's niece, the only family he has left on the island. She
  supplies Peregrine.
- **Voice**: fast, cheerful, slightly greedy.
  - "Whatever was in your hold is mine now, unless you want to buy it back."
  - "Drowning's the bad one. Second worst on the island, they tell me."
  - "I was going to build ships. Now I take them apart. Same tools."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog. Her two-place routine is brain states on
  `time_of_day` with `step_toward` a marker tile at each place.

### Jory Thistlewood

- **Where**: the camp, by a barrow at the path's fork towards the two towns.
- **What he does**: rag-and-bone man. He buys the small spoils of the first hunts:
  rat tails, bat wings, rabbit feet, bones. He pays less than the towns, and says
  so. By day he sits at the barrow; at night he sleeps beside it.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 23. Born on the island. A harbour pickpocket.
- **Backstory**: he likes the island now, because nobody can hang him. He has
  died fourteen times, more than anyone else at the camp, because he once tried
  to rob the warriors' smithy and Brannoc Hale killed him for it, more than
  once.
- **Ties**: Hamid Sarraf taught him to trade. He is scared of Brannoc and polite
  to him.
- **Voice**: quick, cheeky, honest about being dishonest.
  - "Rat tails, bat wings, bones. I pay a little. The towns pay more, but the
    towns are a long way off."
  - "Brannoc killed me four times for one stolen knife. Fair, I suppose."
  - "Twenty-three for a hundred and thirty years. My mother said I'd never grow
    up. She was right."
  - "Human skull? Put that away. Somebody here knew them."
- **Game support**: a buy-only shop dialog (player gives spoils, receives gold).
  A line refusing a human skull needs an `if` on whether the player carries one,
  which the dialog `if` cannot ask; see the gaps.

## The druids' town

Built round the rune temple, on the road from the camp to the deep woods. Every
druid is expected to be able to manifest a flame to cook, light a room or warm
water; they talk about runes as tools.

### Iolanthe Sarno

- **Where**: the druids' town, the council house on the temple square. She sleeps
  in the rooms above the council chamber.
- **What she does**: speaker of the druids' council, and the town's leader. She
  hears complaints in the morning, sits with the council in the afternoon and
  walks the town at dusk. She sells nothing. She tells players what the town
  expects of them and pays a bounty in gold for wolves killed near the garden
  plots. She fights with runes if the town is attacked.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 61. Born on the island. The harbour town's magistrate.
- **Backstory**: when the harbour flooded she led its people inland. That first
  winter Hesper's runes kept them warm and fed, and Iolanthe has backed the
  craft ever since. When Absalom, who had been her captain of the watch, took
  the people who refused runes east, she let them go rather than fight. She
  does not want Wyrd broken until somebody can say what happens to a person
  who should have died of age a hundred years ago. She has died twelve times,
  three of them in the fights before the split.
- **Ties**: Absalom Grieve was her captain of the watch, and she still calls him
  Captain. Hesper Vaun sits on her council. Ragna Holt leads the warriors; the two
  of them meet at the roadside inn twice a year, and Margit keeps the back room
  free for it. Taavi Kask, her day warden, was one of Absalom's watchmen.
- **Voice**: measured, formal, used to being listened to.
  - "Welcome to the temple town. Carve what you like. Don't carve it at anyone."
  - "I led these people up from the harbour. I'm still leading them, a hundred
    and thirty years later. Nobody else has had the chance to try."
  - "Break Wyrd, and Morwen is seventy and Peregrine is sixty, and both of them
    are a hundred and thirty years late. Somebody should think about that before
    they climb."
  - "Twelve deaths. Three of them were our own people, in the year before the
    split. Those are the ones I remember."
- **Game support**: a dialog with a bounty trade (wolf spoils for gold). She
  fights with spells the way Lord Corvin does today: single-target spells only,
  because an area spell would hit the townsfolk round her.

### Hesper Vaun

- **Where**: the druids' town, the rune temple, at the carving table between the
  three forges.
- **What she does**: the runemaster, and an elder of the temple. She is the only
  person who carves runes. A player shapes a blank at one of the forges; she
  takes the blank and an essence and carves the mark, and which rune comes out
  is chance. She also teaches how each forge works and which material it takes.
  She carves through the day; at night she sleeps in a cell behind the temple.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 58. Born on the island. She was Halvard's pupil, one of
  the few people he taught before Maren's sickness took all his time.
- **Backstory**: she was in the lighthouse kitchen the night Maren died and the
  light at the top changed colour. Since then she has carved for everybody and
  taught the temple the craft as he taught it to her, without the parts she
  thinks he got wrong. She has died seven times, each time to something she
  went to fetch: crystal in the frost caves, mostly. What she has lost frightens
  her more than it does other people, because she is afraid of losing the
  carving.
- **Ties**: she and Edda Kell were friends until the towns split, and no longer
  speak. Dagny Rook is her clerk. Aurelio Benedetti was her best pupil before he
  left.
- **Voice**: precise, patient, a teacher. She calls Halvard by his name.
  - "Bring me the blank and the essence. What the mark becomes is not up to
    either of us."
  - "Bone takes the first forge, crystal the second, deep ore the third. Shape it
    there, and bring it here."
  - "Halvard was the best carver who ever lived. He cut one rune too deep, and
    we are all still inside it."
  - "I remember people growing old. I don't miss it, and I would give it back
    tomorrow."
  - "Seven deaths. Each time I check my hands afterwards, to see if they still
    know the marks."
- **Game support**: a dialog for teaching. The carving itself gives a random rune
  from a pool, which a dialog `request_trade` cannot do (it gives fixed items);
  a `craft` with a `one` output does roll by weight. Either her tile carries a
  craft, which needs checking that a battler tile can, or the carving table
  beside her is the crafter and she explains it.

### Dagny Rook

- **Where**: the druids' town, the rune temple, at the clerk's desk by the door.
- **What she does**: temple clerk. She sells crude neutral runes the temple keeps
  ready-carved (Light, Spark and the like), and buys bone and crystal for the
  forges. She keeps the temple's hours: open at dawn, closed at dusk.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 26. Born on the island. A clerk in the harbour master's
  office.
- **Backstory**: she took to runes faster than anyone in the temple's history and
  is impatient to carve, which Hesper will not allow. She has died once, to a
  bog imp, and is ashamed of how little it was.
- **Ties**: she shares a house with Mina Dross. Hesper's clerk. Her father was
  the harbour master, and she will tell anyone who asks that he would have
  hated the lighthouse.
- **Voice**: brisk, eager, a little superior.
  - "Light and Spark are ready-carved. Anything stronger, bring a blank."
  - "One death in a hundred and thirty years. A bog imp. I'd rather not talk
    about it."
  - "Some of the warriors have had a century to learn and still can't light a
    pipe without a flint."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog with racks of runes and a buy rack for bone and
  crystal.

### Amaru Quispe

- **Where**: the druids' town, the weaving house on the temple square.
- **What he does**: weaves caster's robes, hoods and gloves, the gear that helps
  a caster rather than stopping a blade. He buys leather and moss for dye. By
  day he weaves at the window; at night he sleeps upstairs.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 37. He came from a mountain country across the sea and
  had a sailcloth loom on the harbour.
- **Backstory**: he wove his first robe from the last bolt of sailcloth on the
  island. He has died twelve times, eight of them in the frost caves, where he
  goes for the white moss he dyes with.
- **Ties**: Halina Brzeska makes the gear that stops his customers' spells, and
  the two of them argue about it whenever they meet at the inn.
- **Voice**: soft, unhurried, proud of his work.
  - "This one holds a spell better. That one is warmer. You can't have both."
  - "The frost caves killed me eight times. The moss is worth it."
  - "Halina makes coats that stop my customers' spells. I tell her she is
    weaving against me."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog with racks by slot, as armour is racked today.

### Noor Halabi

- **Where**: the druids' town, the apothecary beside the bakehouse.
- **What she does**: apothecary. She buys slime jelly, wisp ash, dew and spores,
  and imp horn, and sells potions. Open day and evening; she sleeps in the back.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 45. She came from abroad as a spice merchant's buyer and
  stayed to run his warehouse on the harbour.
- **Backstory**: she could tell a cargo's worth by smell, and turned the same
  nose to wisp spoils. She has died nineteen times, mostly by poisoning herself
  testing a brew; she keeps notes because each death takes some of what she
  worked out.
- **Ties**: Pell Marrow is her husband. Rosalind Peake keeps a copy of her notes
  in the ledger house. She and Morwen Teague trade bog plants, and neither will
  say so in town.
- **Voice**: dry, exact, quick to correct.
  - "Jelly from a slime, ash from a fire wisp, dew from a water one. Bring them
    separately, please."
  - "Nineteen deaths and most of them my own fault. A poison you've tasted is a
    poison you understand."
  - "I write everything down. The next time I die, I'll need it."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog, buying spoils for cash and selling potions, as
  the potion shop works today.

### Pell Marrow

- **Where**: the druids' town, the bakehouse on the temple square.
- **What he does**: baker. He bakes with a fire rune instead of wood, and sells
  bread, pies and honey cakes. He bakes before dawn, sells through the morning,
  and sleeps in the afternoon. He buys flour, berries and honey.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 31. Born on the island. He baked ship's biscuit for the
  harbour.
- **Backstory**: he was one of the first people outside Halvard's pupils to learn
  runes, so that he could bake without hauling wood. He has died four times,
  which is few, because he rarely leaves the town; one was a troll on the road,
  which he describes in more detail than anybody wants.
- **Ties**: Noor Halabi's husband. He sends bread to the inn every morning with
  Yeva.
- **Voice**: cheerful, talkative, loves describing food.
  - "Baked on a Flame rune, not wood. Tastes cleaner. Don't tell the warriors."
  - "A troll killed me on the road once. It took a long time. I don't walk that
    road any more; Yeva carries my bread."
  - "Four deaths in a hundred and thirty years. I'm careful. I bake."
- **Game support**: a food shop dialog. His afternoon sleep is a brain state on
  `time_of_day` walking to a bed; his dialog cannot change by time of day, so he
  cannot say "come back in the morning" only in the afternoon.

### Suvi Kallio

- **Where**: the druids' town, the garden plots on the town's west side.
- **What she does**: grower. She raises vegetables and fruit trees with nature
  runes and sells apples, greens and roots. She works the plots by day and sits
  on her doorstep in the evening. She pays for rabbit feet, which she buries in
  the plots for luck.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 48. She came from the cold north as a fisherman's wife;
  he drowned years before Wyrd.
- **Backstory**: she had never grown anything and took it up because she was
  sick of fish. She has died nine times, five of them to the wolves that come
  to the edge of the plots from the deep woods.
- **Ties**: Margit Oslund is her closest friend; she visits the inn on market
  days. Orrin Fask built her house.
- **Voice**: plain, practical, a bit sharp.
  - "Apples are a gold. Rabbit feet buy you one free."
  - "I haven't eaten a fish in a hundred years. Good."
  - "The wolves come out of the woods at night. Five times now. They take the
    throat first, if you want to know."
- **Game support**: a food shop dialog.

### Casimir Wren

- **Where**: the druids' town, its streets. He lives in a room over the
  apothecary.
- **What he does**: lamplighter. At dusk he walks the town lighting each lamp post
  with a flick of a Spark rune; at dawn he puts them out. Between rounds he sits
  outside the bakehouse.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 63. He came to the island as a ship's boy fifty years
  before Wyrd and lit the harbour lamps for most of his life.
- **Backstory**: he lit the lamps on the quay the night Wyrd took hold, and says
  he watched the lighthouse light turn from yellow to white. He has died three
  times, all in the first year, and has hardly left the town since.
- **Ties**: Tobiah Marsh is the warriors' lamplighter and was his apprentice on
  the quay; they meet at the inn once a month and argue over whose lamps burn
  longer.
- **Voice**: gentle, a little proud, likes a joke.
  - "Evening. The lamps don't light themselves. Well, they nearly do."
  - "Tobiah over in the warriors' town uses a taper. I taught him that. I've
    learned better since."
  - "The light on the lighthouse is the only lamp on this island I'd put out if

### Mina Dross

### Rosalind Peake

## The warriors' town

On the east side of the island, at the edge of the Red Waste, on the road from the
camp to the lighthouse and the ember deeps. Nobody here touches a rune. They
light fires with flint, train every morning in the drill yard and make gear that
holds off magic.

### Ragna Holt

- **Where**: the warriors' town, the longhall on the main square. She sleeps in
  the room at its far end.
- **What she does**: chief of the warriors' town. She holds the longhall in the
  morning, hears disputes, and sets the watch rota with Absalom. In the
  afternoon she trains with the greataxe. In the evening she eats in the
  longhall with whoever is there. She sells nothing. She keeps the list of
  players who want to climb the lighthouse and tells them who else is looking
  for a group.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 49. Born on the island. She ran the harbour's ropewalk
  and the forty people who worked it.
- **Backstory**: Absalom led the people east, and Ragna built the town. When it
  was done the town asked her to lead it, and Absalom agreed; he says he can
  train people or run them, not both. She wants Wyrd broken, and has decided
  that it will be done by a group, not by one hero, so she spends her time
  putting people together. She has died thirty-eight times, eleven of them on
  the lighthouse.
- **Ties**: Absalom Grieve is her drillmaster and second. Edda Kell sits at her
  table every night. She meets Iolanthe Sarno at the roadside inn twice a year.
  Gideon Okafor worked in her ropewalk.
- **Voice**: loud, direct, warm with her own people.
  - "You want the tower? Good. You can't climb it alone. Nobody can. Give me your
    name and I'll find you three more."
  - "Absalom brought us here. I built the walls. We each did the part we were good
    at."
  - "Eleven times on the tower. Every one of them because somebody behind me ran."
  - "Iolanthe thinks we should wait and see what breaking it does. I've waited a
    hundred and thirty years. I've seen."
- **Game support**: a dialog. She fights with a greataxe and the
  `townsfolk-fight` trait. Her list of climbers may be possible with what the
  game has now, for example a `tag` for each player who signs up. If telling
  a player who else is on the list needs more than that, it is a new feature
  to add later.

### Absalom Grieve

- **Where**: the warriors' town, the drill yard, and the house beside it.
- **What he does**: drillmaster, and second to Ragna Holt. He drills the town at
  dawn, trains anyone who asks, and sets the watch rota in the afternoon. He sells
  nothing, but a player can spar with him. At night he walks the walls.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 46. Born on the island. Captain of the harbour watch.
- **Backstory**: in the first decades, when the arguments over runes turned to
  fights, he led those who refused them east and built the town. He has died
  forty-four times, many of them deliberately, to test himself against the
  lighthouse. He wants Wyrd broken and says so.
- **Ties**: Ragna Holt is the town's chief; he is her second. Iolanthe Sarno was
  his magistrate. Mateo Ferran and Tobiah Marsh served in his watch, and so did
  Taavi Kask, who stayed with the temple. Edda Kell is his
  sister. He dislikes that the inn's cat is called Captain.
- **Voice**: clipped, formal, never raises his voice.
  - "Up at dawn. A body is the one thing on this island no rune made. Train it."
  - "Forty-four times. I learn something every time. Mostly what not to do."
  - "Break the rune at the top of the tower and we all go back to dying once.
    That seems right to me."
  - "The druids are not wicked. They are using the same knife that cut us."
- **Game support**: a dialog. Sparring needs a fight a player starts from dialog
  that stops at a health threshold; brain `health` conditions allow a break-off,
  but starting such a duel from dialog does not exist.

### Brannoc Hale

- **Before Wyrd**: aged 39. Born on the island. He forged harpoons and anchor

  - "Twenty-two times on that road. The waste is hot and the things in it are

- **Where**: the roadside inn. She has the room behind the bar.
- **What she does**: innkeeper. She sells ale, stew and smoked meat, and rents
  beds for the night. She is behind the bar from noon to the small hours. Her
  cat Captain keeps the cellar clear.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 50. Born on the island. She fished from Peregrine Voss's
  boat and kept its accounts.
- **Backstory**: she built the inn halfway between the towns so that both could
  drink in one room. She has died eight times, four of them breaking up fights
  between druids and warriors.
- **Ties**: Captain, a grey tabby. Suvi Kallio is her closest friend. She reminds
  Peregrine who his crew were. Kofi is her brewer. Bertha sells her meat and
  Pell sends her bread.
- **Voice**: warm, tired, firm.
  - "Ale's a gold. A bed's five. Fight outside."
  - "Druids at that end, warriors at this end. Everybody drinks the same ale."
  - "A hundred and thirty years, and I've buried nobody. I've just watched them
    all come back through that door."
- **Game support**: a food shop dialog. Renting a bed as a place to wake is
  outside canon (only the towns and the camp have one); it is offered as a
  proposal. Without that, the bed is only somewhere to stand.

### Kofi Asante

- **Where**: the roadside inn, in the kitchen and cellar. He sleeps in the loft.
- **What he does**: brewer and cook. He brews the ale, cooks the stew, and buys
  honey, grain and raw meat. He works the kitchen through the evening and the
  cellar in the morning.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 28. He came from abroad as a cabin hand and stayed to cook
  in Ifeoma's eating house.
- **Backstory**: he brewed the first ale after the harbour's breweries flooded,
  from wild grain. He has died eleven times; the last was a wolf at the inn
  door, which he has not forgiven the wolves for.
- **Ties**: Ifeoma taught him to cook. He buys Mina's grain.
- **Voice**: easy-going, funny.
  - "Best ale on the island. Only ale on the island, but still."
  - "Eleven times. A wolf at the door, last. At the door!"
  - "Druids heat my ale with a rune if I let them. I don't let them."
- **Game support**: a food shop dialog.

### Rodrigo Vela

- **Where**: the roadside inn, at the same table every night; he rents the room
  over the stable.
- **What he does**: retired adventurer and drinker. He arrives at dusk, drinks ale
  until late, sleeps in his room and walks the lakeshore by day. He tells
  stories for a drink and sells old maps of the dungeons, marked with where he
  died.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 36. He came from abroad as a fisherman and crewed for
  Peregrine Voss.
- **Backstory**: nobody needed fish after the harbour went, so he went into the
  caves. He climbed the lighthouse with Fen Achterberg, mapped the barrow and the
  bear caves, and has died a hundred and sixty times. He stopped when he noticed
  he had forgotten the names of the people he climbed with.
- **Ties**: crewed with Margit for Peregrine. Climbed with Fen.
- **Voice**: expansive, sentimental, slightly drunk.
  - "Buy me an ale and I'll tell you how I got past the first floor."
  - "A hundred and sixty. Each map has a little cross where it happened."
  - "Maren looked at me. That was the worst of it. Not the dying."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog for maps. His dusk-to-dawn drinking and night's
  sleep are brain states on `time_of_day`, walking to his table and to a bed
  (see the two-leg note on stairs in `docs/notes.md`).

## The witch's house

A house on stilts at the edge of the bog, in the Greenwood, away from both towns
and from the road.

### Morwen Teague

- **Where**: the witch's house at the bog's edge.
- **What she does**: the witch. She uses no runes. She brews from bog plants, imp
  horn and the things she catches in the reeds, and sells remedies and brews
  that neither town makes. She buys imp horns and bog plants. She gathers in the
  bog by night and sleeps by day. Her cat Nettle hunts the frogs and rats round
  the stilts.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 70, the oldest-looking person a player will meet. Born on
  the island. She was its healer before runes.
- **Backstory**: she nursed Maren through her sickness, and was with her the night
  she died, before Halvard came down. She believes Maren knew she was dying and
  was ready, and that Halvard took that from her. She left the harbour soon
  after and has lived alone since. She has died thirty times, mostly to the bog
  itself.
- **Ties**: Nettle, a thin black and white cat. Orrin Fask visits her. Noor
  Halabi buys her plants in secret. She will not let Anselm Quaye in.
- **Voice**: old-fashioned, blunt, unnerving, not unkind.
  - "I sat with Maren while she went. She went well. Then he brought her back."
  - "No runes in this house. Wipe your feet."
  - "Thirty times the bog has had me. It gives me back every time, a bit less."
  - "On a still night you can hear her from here, up in the tower. She sings.
    She doesn't know the words."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog. Her nocturnal routine is brain states on
  `time_of_day`.

## The roads

Travellers on the roads between the camp, the two towns, the inn and the
lighthouse. Each walks a fixed route.

### Anselm Quaye

- **What he does**: a pilgrim. He walks a circuit of the island's roads, camp to
  druids to inn to warriors and back, and stops at each place to pray. He sells
  nothing. He gives a blessing and a piece of bread, and asks castaways the
  name of their ship so he can add it to his prayers.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 55. Born on the island. The harbour chapel's lay brother.
- **Backstory**: the chapel flooded with the harbour. He decided the whole island
  is his chapel now. He has died sixty times on the roads, mostly to wolves, and
  once to the cyclops when he walked into the waste to pray for it. He wants to
  climb the lighthouse to pray for Maren, and nobody will take him.
- **Ties**: Rosalind Peake's brother. Morwen Teague will not let him in.
- **Voice**: mild, sincere, unhurried.
  - "Which ship was yours? I'll pray for her."
  - "Sixty times on these roads. I keep walking them."
  - "I married Halvard and Maren, in the old chapel. Somebody should pray for
    her. She's had nobody but him."
- **Game support**: see *Travellers* under the gaps.

### Hamid Sarraf

- **What he does**: a pedlar who walks between the two towns and the camp with a
  pack. He sells small things from one town in the other: druids' honey cakes in
  the warriors' town, warriors' flint in the druids' town. He buys anything
  small. He walks by day and sleeps at the inn.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 47. He came from abroad as a market trader and kept a
  stall on the quay.
- **Backstory**: he chose the road because it lets him belong to neither town. He
  has died twenty-five times, all on the road, and knows every place a wolf
  waits.
- **Ties**: he taught Jory Thistlewood to trade. He and Yeva share the road and
  the gossip.
- **Voice**: smooth, gossipy, a salesman.
  - "Honey cakes from the druids. Flint from the warriors. Same price either
    way."
  - "Twenty-five times on this road. There's a bend after the lake. Walk wide."
  - "Neither town is wrong. I tell both of them so, and sell to both of them."
- **Game support**: a shop dialog; see *Travellers* under the gaps.

### Yeva Kostenko

- **What she does**: courier. She runs letters and parcels between the camp, the
  two towns and the inn: Pell's bread to the inn, Rukmini's letters to Mina. A
  player can give her a parcel to carry, or carry one for her for gold. She runs
  by day and sleeps at whichever end she reaches at dusk.
- **Before Wyrd**: aged 22. She came from abroad on a mail packet and ran messages
  for the harbour master.
- **Backstory**: she has died five times on the road, once to a troll that had
  come out of the bear caves, and she runs faster since.
- **Ties**: she carries letters between Rukmini and Mina. Shares the road with
  Hamid.
- **Voice**: breathless, quick, cheerful.
  - "Letter for the druids? Give it here, I'm going that way."
  - "A troll, on the road. I've run faster ever since."
  - "A hundred and thirty years and my legs still think they're twenty-two.
    That part I don't mind."
- **Game support**: carrying a parcel is a `tag` set by one NPC's dialog and read
  by another's `if`, which the dialog supports today. Her running is a
  traveller's route; see the gaps.

## What the game cannot do yet

What the profiles above ask for that the game does not support today, and what
each would need.

- **Townsfolk drop their kit.** A killed townsperson wakes again after 30 to 60
  minutes (`respawn`), but drops their whole kit as well as their skull. The
  canon says they drop only the skull.
- **An NPC's dialog cannot change by time of day.** The dialog `if` asks only
  about the player (a tag, a channel, a rating). A different line at night, or
  "come back in the morning", needs a `time_of_day` leaf in dialog conditions.
- **An NPC's dialog cannot ask what the player carries.** Refusing a human skull
  (Jory, Magda) or turning a rune-carrier away at the gate (Edda) needs a
  `carrying` leaf in dialog conditions. Brains already have `carrying`.
- **An NPC cannot remember its own deaths.** Lines about how many times a
  character has died are fixed text. If the count should rise when a player
  kills them, the dialog needs to read the NPC's own death count.
- **Rosalind cannot read a player's deaths.** The server keeps a `death` row for
  each character death, but dialog cannot see it.
- **The runemaster's carve is random.** A dialog trade gives fixed items. A
  `craft` with a `one` output rolls by weight; whether an NPC battler tile can
  also carry a craft needs checking.
- **Cats.** Each named cat is its own tile, copied from the shared cat and drawn
  in one of the coats from `bun run generate:cats`: Biscuit in the shared cat's
  ginger, Soot black, Ghee cream, Captain grey and Nettle tuxedo.
- **Travellers.** A brain finds a goal only as far as its furthest distance
  (`brainReach`), and the towns are about 340 cells apart by road. A traveller needs a
  chain of waypoint markers along the road, each a distinct tile, with a brain
  state per leg, or a new action that follows a named route. Routing does not
  open doors, so routes should not cross one.
- **Animal skulls do not exist.** No creature drops a skull today. Magda's trade
  needs a skull spoil per animal, or one generic `animal-skull`.
- **Sparring.** Absalom offering a fight from dialog that stops before death does
  not exist.
- **Ragna's list of climbers** may need a new feature if it has to tell a player
  who else signed up; a `tag` per player may be enough.
- **Waking at the inn.** Only the towns and the camp are places to wake in the
  canon. A bed at the inn as a place to wake is a proposal.
- **A sprite per character.** Every named NPC is a tile and needs its own sprite;
  see `.agents/skills/sprite-design/`.

## Open points against the canon

- **Why castaways survive the wrecks**, and what happens to everybody else on the
  ships, is left open. No character explains it.
- **The old harbour town** on Wreckers' Bay, the lighthouse built to guide ships
  into it, and the split of its people into the two towns are new facts,
  offered as proposals.
- **How long Wyrd has held.** This doc uses about 130 years.
- **Halvard and Maren's life before Wyrd.** Hesper Vaun as his pupil, Orrin Fask
  building the lower floors, Edda Kell keeping the door, Morwen Teague nursing
  Maren and Anselm Quaye marrying them are new facts. Whether Halvard was born
  on the island is left open.
- **Nobody was a child when Wyrd took hold.** The cast assumes this so that the
  canon's "no children on the island" holds.
