# Terrain, movement and sight

- `settlePlates` (`app/game/pressurePlates.ts`) and `settleSignals` (`app/game/signals.ts`) each run one settle pass per call, not a loop to a fixed point. Plates or receivers wired to trigger each other oscillate once per tick instead of hanging a tick; a cascade resolves over the following ticks.
- A queued step must be takeable in the same tick the previous step commits (`GameSession.requestStep`). Otherwise every cell costs an extra tick and the server falls behind the client's prediction.
- `GameSession.advanceMotion` ends a walk with `reached` (`app/game/ticks.ts`), not a plain `>=`. `TICK_MS` is not exact, so twelve ticks add up to 399.99999999999994ms, and a 400ms step (a player wading in water, or the cat) would commit a tick after `RemoteSession` lands its prediction of the same step on the frame clock.
- A tile with a `footprint` is stored as one placement per covered cell: the anchor in the south-east cell and a part (carrying `span`) in each other cell. `settleSpans` (`app/lib/footprint.ts`) is the only writer of parts and runs on every assignment to `GameSession.map` and every editor `commitMap`. Removing any cell removes all of them; a change to the tile, facing or variant of any cell is copied to the rest and re-cuts the footprint; a change a part's cell cannot take (`canReplaceStack`) is undone in every cell. Because of the setter, a `GameSession` edit has to be derived from `this.map`, not from a value already assigned to it, or the parts it settled are lost.
- A part carries only `tileId`, `direction` and `variant`. Anything that reads a placement's other fields resolves a part to its anchor first (`spanAnchor`), as `pickTopAt`, `interact` and `canInteract` do. Renderers and the light bake skip parts, so the tile is drawn and lit once, from the anchor.
- Nothing moves a footprint yet: `pushDestination` refuses a column holding a `span`. Moving only the anchor would make `settleSpans` remove the old footprint and place a new one, and whether those overlap depends on the order they settle in.
