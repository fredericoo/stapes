"the-last-stones": patch
---

A cave troll you hit from water it cannot wade into now backs away out of sight instead of standing on the bank. Wisps stop chasing you once you have led them far enough from where they drift, unless you keep hitting them. Town guards attack every monster that comes near, and guards and shopkeepers no longer walk home while somebody is still hitting them. A door that closes on you or on a creature pushes whoever is in the doorway out of it. Monsters fill the diagonal squares around you when the four beside you are taken, so standing inside a ring of bats no longer keeps a troll or a wolf away.
