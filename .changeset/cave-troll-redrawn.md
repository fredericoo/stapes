The cave troll is redrawn as a stooped, basalt-grey brute with tusks and live coals smouldering along its back, and three more trolls now live above ground. Trolls roam the northern and western wilds; they sleep through the day and run when they are losing. River trolls wait on the banks of the river that runs through the western woods, swim after you and ice over when hurt. Moss trolls sit in the deep woods as still as boulders and take root to mend.
