The cave troll is redrawn as a stooped, basalt-grey brute with tusks and live coals smouldering along its back. Three more trolls exist but are not yet placed in the world: a plain troll that sleeps through the day and runs when it is losing, a river troll that swims after you and ices over when hurt, and a moss troll that sits still as a boulder and takes root to mend.
