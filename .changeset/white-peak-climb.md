The snowy mountain has been rebuilt. Caves open on every side of it and climb inside all the way to the yeti on the summit, getting harder the higher you go, with deer and slimes at its foot. Wolves are far fewer, a few bears roam the snow, and there is an abandoned camp with a diary, chests to find, and a crevasse you can only reach by dropping from the top.
