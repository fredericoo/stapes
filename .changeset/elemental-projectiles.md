Neutral, frost and nature spells now look like their element in flight, and stronger stones throw bigger spells. Spark, Sleet and Barbs throw a small star, snowflake or thorn; Bolt, Frost and Thorns throw a comet, ice shard or tumbling leaf; Lance, Rime and Bramble throw a violet orb, frozen orb or tumbling bramble. Each leaves a trail and bursts in its own colours when it lands.
