At the bottom of the pit beneath the white peak's summit stands a ruined temple with no door, and in its heart a chest holds the Glacier, a legendary rune of clear ice in a gold rim. Cast it and a block of ice half a storey high rises in front of you, the only way to climb out of the pit and over the frozen lip a full storey high that rings the summit, before it melts away.
