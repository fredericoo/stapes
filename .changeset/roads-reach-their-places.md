"the-last-stones": minor
---

The roads out of the camp now all start at one crossroads just north of it, where a signpost lists every direction, and every place a road leads to has a sign with its name; the sign at the roadside inn lists its directions too. The road from the inn to the frost caves now crosses the lake by bridges between islands instead of stopping on one, the road from the fishing hamlet now goes in through a new west gate of the druids' town, and a road that crosses a river at a slant now bridges straight across it instead of laying the bridge down the river.
