# the-last-stones

## 0.26.0

### Minor Changes

- The world is a new, much larger island, with towns, a lighthouse, caves, dungeons and a mountain to explore. Everyone wakes at its spawn once, keeping what they carry and what they have learnt.
- An archer drawing its bow on you now shows a red dotted line to you, the same warning a spell being cast at you gives, so you can step out of its sight before the arrow flies. The shadow under these lines now falls on the ground beneath them, following cliffs and slopes, instead of sitting a pixel under the dots.
- Boars and raccoons now roam the western forest. A boar leaves you alone until you come within a few steps, then charges anyone who backs away; a raccoon makes off with food you drop and runs from anyone strong. Idris Kaya and Jory Thistlewood buy their tusks and tails.
- The first camp's pub is now a proper two-storey inn: drinks downstairs, beds upstairs. Idris has a tanning rack by his pitch, and Runa practises on a training dummy when there is nothing to shoot, though a rat in the camp still comes first.
- You can now make your own runes. Shape bone, crystal shards or obsidian shards into a blank at the rune temple's forges, then ask Hesper Vaun to carve it, with an essence for a fire, water or nature rune or without one for a plain rune; which rune you get is chance. Every creature of an element now sometimes drops that element's essence, and creatures no longer drop runes. Craft windows show each recipe's name, cost and possible results on their own lines, and a craft with a fee takes any coin, gold bars included, and gives change.
- Cats now come in seven more coats: black, grey, white, brown, cream, tuxedo and blue.
- Cats now pounce when they spot a rat, darting at it faster than it can run for a couple of seconds before they need to catch their breath.
- Cobras now hunt the desert and anacondas lie in wait in the bog and the deep woods. A cobra rears and spits venom that poisons and dazes you before it closes to bite; an anaconda ignores you until you step right beside it, then squeezes you still and crushes you, and hits about as hard as a cave troll. Both usually drop two snake fangs.
- Most creatures you kill now leave something behind: rat tails, bat wings, snake fangs, bear claws, slime jelly, wisp dust and more. Sell them for gold: animal parts and hides to the tanner, and jelly and wisp dust to the potion salesman.
- The world editor has new deep-woods pieces: a broad old tree with a dark crown and roots, a fallen tree with its roots torn up, a tree stump, ferns that lie flat when you walk through them, and undergrowth and tree roots that slow you down.
- The druids' town is now more than twice the size, and its people have moved in. It is ringed by trees instead of a stone wall, its houses are thatched, and a brook runs through it under four wooden bridges to a lake past the south gate. Stone stairs climb to a raised quarter in the north-east. The rune temple stands on a square in the middle, with the council house, the weaving house, the ledger house, the bakehouse, the apothecary and the granary round it, each with a sign at its door. Every shop on the island now has lanterns inside, so none is dark. Mirela Dobos keeps the depot, open at one corner, day and night, and explains how its chests work. There are also garden plots and an orchard, a builder's yard by the long house, and rows of cottages. The other fourteen townsfolk keep their shops and posts by day and go home to their own beds at night. Taavi Kask at the temple door can tell you where each shop is, and Casimir Wren lights twelve lamp posts at dusk and puts them out after sunrise. The depot in the druids' town is now the only one: the silver chests in the bat cave, the bear den and the ice caves are gone, and anything you kept in them can no longer be reached.
- Some gear now raises a mastery while you wear it, and the Wolfskin Hood, Coat and Boots each raise Nature, so a full set makes your nature spells hit harder, heal more and cast faster. The bonus shows on the item and beside the mastery in your stats, and it never counts towards what you can wield or earn experience.
- Shops now trade in gold instead of arcane shards. Gold coins pile up to 100, and a gold bar is worth 100 gold; you pay with your largest coins first and get change back in gold. The blacksmith buys the arcane shards you mine for one gold each.
- You now wake at the water's edge beside the wreck, by a fire, with Tove there to send you up the road. Ask her to trade to see what she buys and sells; she now takes rat tails and slime jelly too. The camp is much closer: a short walk up a valley between steep hills, past a small ruin, with rats, small slimes, rabbits, deer, a bat cave and a couple of snakes keeping to the edges. Rats no longer gang up on you, and the camp's chests and barrels hold only a little food. The camp is now a pub and a ring of tents round the fire, everyone there sleeps on a bedroll, and Runa, the camp's archer, shoots anything with teeth that comes close. The old harbour's warehouse cellar has a few rats and a slime in it, and much less food. Somewhere along the sea cliffs east of the wreck there is a hidden cave worth wading to.
- Houses in the map builder can have a railed porch on any side, set out as a seating area, a smith's forge and anvil, or a drill yard with training dummies, and a floor can now be furnished as a smithy or a barracks.
- Ifeoma's stew now simmers in an iron cauldron by the camp fire, and you can smell it from across the camp. The townsfolk's cats are protected like their owners: you need PvP on to hurt one, and if you hit Biscuit, Ifeoma will come after you.
- Lanterns and candles can be placed in the world and lit or put out by hand. A lantern stands on the floor or hangs on a wall, and a candle sits on a table or a barrel. Townsfolk can be given lights to look after: they light the ones near them at dusk and put them out when their hours end.
- A lava chasm now opens in the desert floor over the obsidian deeps. Each of the dungeon's five levels is a basalt cavern around it, with cave mouths in the cliffs. Drop from ledge to ledge past lavafalls to the lava lake at the bottom, and climb back out by the dungeon's stairs.
- Lava has arrived. It glows in the dark, slows you to half speed and sets you burning while you stand in it. Black cooling crust and dark basalt are added for the ground around it.
- The lighthouse can now be built as a tower many storeys high: rubble and limewashed stone walls with arrow slits and doorways, ledges where it narrows, pedestals that open doors when something is set on them, an open arcade and a crenellated parapet for the top, a stone roof, and Wyrd itself, a crimson rune that beats like a heart on its plinth.
- The old trees of the deep woods now grow to different heights with four different crowns, and the trunk and crown above you lift out of the way when you walk behind one, showing the rings of the cut trunk.
- Old ruins can now be overgrown: hanging vines, climbing ivy and moss can be added to stone walls and pillars, flagstone floors can have moss and grass breaking through, and piles of rubble lie where walls have collapsed.
- Cliffs can now be built from frosted rock with snow on its ledges, from banded desert sandstone, or from near-black basalt columns, as well as the red rock every hill has today. Caves leave each of them through a mouth cut in the same rock.
- Respawn points work again: stand on one and choose Set respawn point, and every life after that starts there until you pick another. The one you are bound to glows bright for you; the rest stay dim.
- The harbour pier now stands on posts above the water, and new jetties and boardwalks reach out from the fishing hamlet, the witch's bog, the lighthouse, the lake and the western stones. Climb a ladder or stairs to get onto them, and watch your step: some have rotted through.
- The roads out of the camp now all start at one crossroads just north of it, where a signpost lists every direction, and every place a road leads to has a sign with its name; the sign at the roadside inn lists its directions too. The road from the inn to the frost caves now crosses the lake by bridges between islands instead of stopping on one, the road from the fishing hamlet now goes in through a new west gate of the druids' town, and a road that crosses a river at a slant now bridges straight across it instead of laying the bridge down the river.
- Runa at the camp will sell you her old bow for 10 gold, once, and tell you to practise on the deer and rabbits.
- The people of Rune Isle now live in its towns, the camp, the roadside inn and the witch's house. Each one keeps their own hours at their post, and many walk home to bed at night. Biscuit, Soot, Ghee, Captain and Nettle live with their owners.
- Rune Isle has its wildlife back. Rats, deer, rabbits and bats live round the camp, and each elemental land starts with weak creatures at its edge and keeps the strongest for its centre: slimes on the beaches, the desert edge and the forest edge, wolves and wisps further in, snow wolves and a yeti up the mountain, bears in their caves and imps in the bog. The caves get harder the deeper you go. Trolls keep to their own cave and the deepest floors of the Ember deeps, and the road from the beach to the camp is still safe.
- Grass now grows over the ground instead of being a ground of its own. It spreads over sand and snow with a ragged, shaded edge, and paths, yards and river banks are simply bare ground where the grass stops. Little tufts of grass are dotted about the meadows. The snow starts higher up a much steeper peak, a river plunges three storeys off it in one great waterfall, and forest paths are one tile wide and run straight onto the middle of every bridge. Cliffs stacked on cliffs are solid rock, with no ground showing between them.
- The world is Rune Isle: you wake on the beach beside a wreck below a snow-capped peak, with the Greenwood and its bog to the west, the Red Waste and its lava gorge to the east, and Wreckers' Bay in the south. A safe path leads up from the beach to the camp, and roads run on to the druids' town round the rune temple, the walled warriors' town, a roadside inn on the lake, the witch's house by the bog, and the lighthouse above the drowned ruins of the old harbour.
- The townsfolk keep hours now: most go to bed at night and their shops are shut until morning, the baker sleeps through the afternoon, and the lamplighters and night wardens sleep by day. They step about their posts and call out to you as you pass. Cats chase rats, eat scraps they find, come when you say "psps", and run from you if you are stronger than them, but fight back if you are not.
- The townsfolk are now the people of Rune Isle. Each has a name, a trade and a life from before the curse, and will tell you about it, including how many times they have died. Shops have new keepers: Brannoc Hale forges swords, axes and maces, Selin Arslan sells bows, Halina Brzeska sells armour and Idris Kaya sells leather. Nobody buys arcane shards any more.
- Rune Isle now has caves to explore, six floors deep. Each land has its own way down: the frost caves and the frozen lake under the White Peak, the rootways under the deep woods, the barrow and the flooded caves in the heartland, a sea grotto under the beach, and the Ember deeps under the Red Waste, which end in a lava cavern. A long tunnel deep underground joins them all, and smaller caves by the camp, in the woods and at the desert edge are waiting for the creatures that will live in them.
- The rune temple has new art: a bone forge, a crystal forge and an obsidian forge, obsidian veins for the deepest dungeon floors, and the runemaster, an elder druid in a green robe and hood.
- Arcane stones now look like runes: the material shows how strong one is (bone, crystal or obsidian), its colour shows its element, and a carved mark tells each one apart. Each rune, blank and essence now says on its item card what it is.
- Shelves are now bookshelves, filled with books in three arrangements and turned to face the room. Open one to see the books on it, and take one down to read what is written in it.
- The beach has a wreck to wake beside: a broken hull, a ship's ribs in the shallows, a snapped mast with its torn sail, planks, driftwood and a coil of rope, on beach sand the tide has left damp.
- Rivers now run down cliffs as waterfalls where they drop a level. Ramps on the snowfields are covered in snow, and basalt hilltops stay dark rock right to the cliff edge instead of ending in a ring of sand.
- Drag a pile onto another pile of the same thing to fill it up, even in the same bag; whatever does not fit stays where it was. To split a pile, drag it onto the space that appears under its bag and choose how many to take off. On a computer, Shift-drag moves one and Alt-drag moves half.
- Tove calls "Another one off the wreck! Over here, by the fire." when she sees someone she hasn't met, and stops once you've talked to her. Offers where you give nothing now say Accept instead of Trade.
- Tove now shoots any creature that comes too close to the wreck beach, and tells you more about the island: why ships keep wrecking here, why nobody stays dead, where the camp is and the two towns beyond it. She no longer trades; the camp is where you buy and sell.
- The people of Rune Isle now go to their own beds at night, upstairs and across town included. They open doors and climb ladders on the way, and shut the door behind them. Iolanthe and Rosalind now go to bed too.
- A wood now grows along the road from the wreck to the first camp. It is thick against the hills and thin beside the road, and slimes keep to the deep part of it. No berry bushes grow in the wood, and the island's meadows have half as many as before.
- Anyone wading through shallow water now stands in a ring of dark water that wobbles around them, so a rat crossing a stream no longer vanishes and it is clear when you are knee-deep.
- Pointing at ground you can walk to now frames that cell and shows a pair of footprints beside the cursor, so you can see where a click will take you before you click.
- Water now flows. Rivers run downhill to the sea, lakes carry the river through them, and the sea's waves roll in towards every shore.
- Armour no longer decides a fight by itself. A blow is now weighed against your defence instead of having it subtracted: light armour takes most of a small bite and only a little of a heavy swing, and no amount of armour makes a creature harmless. Light armour protects a little more than it did. Heavy armour no longer shuts out creatures entirely, so expect to take some damage from things you used to shrug off.
  
  Skeletons and bog imps no longer swing broad axes or shoot hunting bows, which could kill in one blow. They fight with lighter weapons and still drop the heavy ones as often as before. Skeletons have more health to make up for it.
- Wenna at the camp now sells a salvaged hammer and axe for 15 gold each instead of sacks, so you can arm yourself before reaching the warriors' town.

### Patch Changes

- Bedrolls now lie flat on the ground in the same view as everything else, and can be laid out facing any of the four directions.
- Everybody wakes on the wreck beach of Rune Isle the first time they come back, with everything they carried and everything they had learnt.
- The cyclops is redrawn as a hulking, hunched cave giant with one great blue eye, fists the size of its head, and a heavy stride.
- Deer have a new look: a russet stag with antlers, drawn at the same angle as the wolf and the boar, with a four-step trot in place of the old two-frame walk.
- Creatures are spread across the whole island now, harder the further you go from the camp. The roads to both towns are clear of wolves. The meadows, shores and groves have rats, snakes, slimes and wisps by night. Wolves, skeletons and the odd bear keep to the northern wilds.
- Fog only rolls in at the top of the mountain, and rain no longer falls on the desert or the snowfields.
- A pile of bones, apples, bread, meat, cheese, mushrooms, skins or arcane stones on the ground now shows as a heap, the way berries already did, instead of a single one.
- Pointing at a heap of items now draws one clean outline around the pile, instead of a white blot that covered most of it.
- Fences no longer join onto the walls beside them.
- The islanders now talk like themselves. Wenna haggles in harbour Cornish, Mina can't stop asking what dying feels like, Fen forgets he already told you about Maren, Rodrigo hiccups through his stories, and every shopkeeper says "anything else?" in their own way.
- Reconnecting the moment you enter the world no longer leaves your character stuck in place, unable to walk.
- Clicking the respawn point you are already bound to now says "You already respawn here." instead of a garbled no-room message.
- The world no longer hitches when a tree, bush or creature grows back, and it no longer freezes for several seconds after a restart while everything that was due grows back at once.
- The road from the camp to the inn now climbs onto the hill north-west of the camp and runs through the bat cave under the crag, with a cave mouth on each side. The road from the crossroads to the ice caves goes round the corner of a hill instead of running into its cliff.
- Rivers and ponds now have rounded sandy banks, and grass ends in soft curves instead of square steps, so a river running at an angle winds instead of zigzagging.
- Rune Isle's rivers now run unbroken, and on grass they run between sandy banks with no grass under the water. In the snow and the desert they run on bare snow and sand. Swamps, towns and ruins fade into the land round them instead of ending in neat ovals and rectangles. The stray patches of snow are gone, and the desert and the snowfields are mostly open ground, with only a few cacti, dunes and pines.
- The showcase island is tidier: no more strips at a cliff's foot that trees close in, the oasis is clear water ringed with grass, the desert and the snowy peak are more open, and hill edges are more ragged.
- Snakes are redrawn: still small and slender, now with a head, gold eyes, a tongue that flicks out while they wait, and a body that winds as they slither.
- Tove no longer goes to bed at night, so if you wash up on the wreck after dark she is still there to feed you and point you up the road.
- Townsfolk working out a long way home through doors and up ladders no longer make the world stall for a moment; they may stand still for up to a second before they set off.
- The hills either side of the road from the wreck now step down in ragged terraces, and their tops have trees, rocks and patches of sand.
- The world stutters less while you walk away from the beach: the land ahead of you is loaded in far less time, so the game keeps its pace.
- The edge of the water now matches the land it lies in: snow runs right up to a mountain stream and mud up to the bog, while rivers through grass keep their sandy banks. On the grassy hills, trails and rivers now wear through to sand just as they do on the ground below.
- Water no longer freezes in place after a while: rivers and the sea keep flowing when someone new comes into view or changes what they wear.
- Water no longer shows reflections. They came out striped under walls on cliffs and as grey smudges under people and creatures, so they are gone until they can be done well.
- Rivers now run straight into their waterfalls, and the pool at the foot of a fall meets the cliff, without a strip of sand across the water on either side of the drop.
- Wolves and snow wolves are redrawn, with a heavier build, jointed legs and a proper trot.
- Woods are open enough to walk through now: trees stand apart instead of in solid walls, and the scrub at their edges is thinner. Old trees grow only in the thickest woods, and some woods have a few dead trees and stumps among the living ones.
- The world now starts fresh from its map every time it restarts, so anything left lying on the ground is cleared away and creatures and harvests are back where they began. If the place you logged out from no longer exists, you wake on the nearest solid ground or at the spawn.

## 0.24.1

### Patch Changes

## 0.24.0

### Minor Changes

- On a computer, your spells now sit in their own bar beside the board instead of in the sidebar. On a tablet or a phone turned sideways, the board takes the full height of the screen with the joystick on one side and your toolbar and nearby list on the other.

### Patch Changes

- Your Arcane now adds to your rating on top of your best weapon, instead of counting only when it was the higher of the two. Creatures are rated the same way, so most of them show a few more stars, and the skeleton, cave troll, yeti and cyclops show a lot more.
- A shield now looks right in either hand. It is turned to the side of the hand holding it and carried in front of the body, where before a shield in the weapon hand sat where a sword would.
- A spell with a cast time is never instant any more. Mastering it brings it down to a fifth of its time and no further.
- You now do one thing at a time: while you cast a spell or harvest, you throw no blows, and your swing winds up again afterwards.

### Patch Changes

### Patch Changes

## 0.21.0

### Minor Changes

- Bears are tougher, and backing off no longer keeps you safe: a bear charges at anyone who keeps their distance, and flies into a rage when badly hurt.
- Burning now lasts two seconds after you step out of a fire instead of piling up for most of a minute. A Flame cast in front of a tree or bush now lands under it, and while it lasts it sets alight what burns right next to it. Food poisoning now shows as Nauseous.

### Patch Changes

- The bear has been redrawn with a shoulder hump, a head carried low, and legs that bend as it walks.
- The north and south gate guards no longer tell you to stay out. They let you through and warn you only about how far it is safe to walk.
- Rats squeak now: they eek when they bolt from you, shriek when they bite, squeak at food and chatter quietly to each other in a pack. Every creature and spell sound is now the noise itself rather than a description in asterisks, so a wolf goes awooo instead of *howl*.

## 0.18.0

### Minor Changes

- Apple trees now grow in the orchard, planted in rows of mud, and a few stand among the trees of the forest. Every few minutes one drops an apple on the ground beside it for you, or anything hungry nearby, to eat. Apples rot after an hour or so, and a rotten apple can make you sick.
- The beaches now have surf: foam washes in and out along the waterline over a strip of wet sand, and the shore curves round its corners instead of meeting the sand in square steps.
- Bears are a new animal, nearly as dangerous as a cave troll. A fed bear sleeps in its den and can be walked past. A hungry one comes out to hunt deer, rabbits and you, and to pick berry bushes. Bears are weak to fire.
- Mushrooms now grow in patches in the caves and inside the flower rings where wisps appear. Pick a patch for a few mushrooms and it grows back after a few minutes. A red mushroom keeps you fed for a short while, a blue one is poisonous, and both stack to 32.
- There is a frozen island off the east coast now. Cross the ice over the strait to reach it: snow slows you a little, ice speeds you up, and it is foggy but never rains. Snow wolves hunt in packs among the pines, water slimes keep to the frozen lakes, tide wisps drift over the open snow, and a yeti waits inside a ring of ice spikes at the island's heart.
- The bat caves in the hills now open through an arch in the hillside, with the grass running unbroken over it.
- Six caves now open into the taller hills. Follow a tunnel in from the cliff face to find red and grey stalagmites and the bats that roost among them.
- You can no longer eat or drink something when one of its effects would not fit: food is refused while you are too full for it, and poison while you are too poisoned. The Eat option and the item in your bag are greyed out and say why. Creatures follow the same rule.
- Hills now have sloped, rounded sides instead of vertical cliffs.

### Patch Changes

- Berries now heal 1 health when eaten, like apples.
- Creatures keep to the places they would live: snakes bask in the open and in the desert instead of wolves, wolves stay under cover deep in the woods, and on the ice island the water slimes and tide wisps gather by the frozen lakes.
- Bats and wisps now fly up and down as well as across. A bat that follows you off a ledge dives after you instead of hanging in the air where it left the ground, and one below you can fly up to reach you.
- Paths no longer show a line of grass where they meet sand, and deep pools no longer show specks of their bed along the rim.
- New ramps climb the hills where the way round was long, and onto two hilltops you could not reach before.
- The world no longer goes down when a new version of the map arrives, now that the map is big enough to include the frozen isle.
- Dirt paths, mud and swamp beds no longer show dark corners and a dark rim where they meet grass or a floor.
- A bush you have picked now casts shade like a full one, instead of letting light through until it grows back.
- The whole island and the frozen isle now meet the sea on sandy beaches, with surf washing up along the shore. Rivers and the northern lake keep their grassy banks.
- The edges of the woods are thinned out, so the paths between hills and clearings connect instead of ending in a wall of trees.

- Rain now puts out campfires and arcane flames left under open sky. A fire under a roof, in a cave or outside the rain keeps burning.

## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- Creatures now find longer ways round to reach you, such as up a ramp to the ledge you are shooting from, so standing one level up no longer keeps you safe.
- Wolves, cats, bats, skeletons, wisps, the bog imp, the cyclops, Lord Corvin, the guards and the townsfolk who fight back now back away out of your sight when you hit them from somewhere they cannot reach. Rats and snakes still stand there and take it.
- Rain and fog can now be kept to parts of the map, so a storm can stop where the desert begins and fog can stay near the swamp.
- The world now has weather. It rains on everything under open sky, and fog closes in so you can only see a few steps around you. Both stay out of caves and houses.
- What a creature drops when it dies now disappears after 10 minutes if nobody picks it up. Anything you carry away is yours to keep.
- Fireflies now come out over the swamp's reeds at night, leaves fall from trees during the day and pollen drifts over flowers.
- The island is no longer flat. Hills and mountains now rise out of the forest and the desert, with ramps leading up them, and every place you could walk to before can still be reached. The town is as level as it was.
- Attack someone in town and anyone who sees it runs to the guards shouting your name. The guards then hunt you down, the town's fighters turn on you, and everyone else keeps clear of you for five minutes, until you die, or until you get away, wait out the fighting and turn PvP off.
- Shopkeepers, guards and the other people of the town now come back 30 to 60 minutes after they are killed, instead of being gone for good.

### Patch Changes

- Apples, bread, cheese, ale and cooked meat left out around the world now grow back a few minutes after you take them, as berries and apple pies already did.
- Far fewer trees drop leaves now, so a forest by day is calmer, with only the odd tree shedding.
- The creature you have targeted keeps its name, health bar and aiming line when it walks behind a pillar, a wall or into the dark, as long as it is on screen.

## 0.15.1

### Patch Changes

- Flickering torches no longer cost a slow frame each time they flicker, and a light you put out no longer leaves its glow on the ground until you walk on.
- A cave troll you hit from water it cannot wade into now backs away out of sight instead of standing on the bank. Wisps stop chasing you once you have led them far enough from where they drift, unless you keep hitting them.
